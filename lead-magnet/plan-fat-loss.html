Before you start

How to use this plan

Your week

Do a circuit three days a week with a rest day in between, for example Monday, Wednesday, Friday. Do Workout A, then B, then C.

Walk every day. Aim for 8,000 steps; a 10-minute walk after each meal gets you most of the way.

How a circuit works

Do each exercise for the set time, rest, then move to the next. After the last exercise, rest 90 seconds. That is one round.

The week-by-week page tells you the times and how many rounds to do.

How hard?

Breathe fast, but still be able to say a short sentence. Pick dumbbells you can move with good form for the whole set; most women start with 2 to 4 kg.

Warm-up (5 minutes)

  • March on the spot, 1 minute
  • Arm circles, 30 seconds each way
  • Hip circles, 30 seconds each way
  • Bodyweight squats x 10
  • Glute bridges x 10
  • Cat-cow x 6

Cool-down (5 minutes)

  • Slow walk, 2 minutes
  • Hamstring stretch, 30 seconds each leg
  • Hip flexor stretch, 30 seconds each side
  • Chest stretch in a doorway, 30 seconds
  • Child's pose, 30 seconds

Safety first

Check with your doctor before you start, especially if you are pregnant or have a medical condition or injury. Stop if anything hurts (effort is fine, pain is not).

Week by week

Build a little each week

Week 1 · Learn the circuits

Do 2 rounds. Work for 30 seconds and rest for 30, so you can focus on form. Measure your waist and write down your dumbbell weights.

Week 2 · Add a round

Do 3 rounds, still 30 seconds on and 30 off. Keep walking every day.

Week 3 · Work a little longer

Do 3 rounds of 40 seconds on and 20 off. If a move feels easy, go up one step in weight.

Week 4 · Finish strong

Do 4 rounds of 40 seconds on and 20 off. On your last day, measure your waist again and compare it with week 1.

Food habits

Eat for steady fat loss

No crash diets and no skipped meals. Aim to lose about half a kilo a week: slower loss is the kind that stays off.

1. Protein at every meal

Dal, paneer, curd, eggs, chicken, fish, sprouts or tofu. Aim for a palm-sized portion each meal.

2. Half a plate of vegetables

At lunch and dinner. Any kind counts: poriyal, salad, sabzi, soup.

3. A little less rice or roti

Keep them, just serve one scoop less than usual and fill the space with vegetables.

4. Drink water, not sugar

2 to 3 litres of water a day. Halve the sugar in your tea and coffee.

5. Plan your snacks

Fruit, roasted chana, buttermilk or a small bowl of curd, so 4 pm hunger doesn't win.

Track more than the scale

Weight moves with water and hormones. Each week also check your waist, a photo and how your clothes fit.
