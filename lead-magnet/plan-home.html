Before you start

How to use this plan

Your space

A space about two metres square, a mat or a folded towel, a sturdy chair and a wall. That is all you need.

Your week

Train three days a week with a rest day in between, for example Monday, Wednesday, Friday. Do Workout A, then B, then C.

Rest 45 to 60 seconds between sets. On the other days, walk: aim for 7,000 to 8,000 steps.

Make it harder without weights

Take 3 seconds to lower. Pause at the bottom. Add 2 reps. Each one makes a bodyweight move feel heavier.

Warm-up (5 minutes)

  • March on the spot, 1 minute
  • Arm circles, 30 seconds each way
  • Hip circles, 30 seconds each way
  • Bodyweight squats x 10
  • Glute bridges x 10
  • Cat-cow x 6

Cool-down (5 minutes)

  • Slow walk, 2 minutes
  • Hamstring stretch, 30 seconds each leg
  • Hip flexor stretch, 30 seconds each side
  • Chest stretch in a doorway, 30 seconds
  • Child's pose, 30 seconds

Safety first

Check with your doctor before you start, especially if you are pregnant or have a medical condition or injury. Stop if anything hurts (effort is fine, pain is not).

Week by week

Build a little each week

Week 1 · Learn the moves

Do 2 sets of each exercise instead of 3. Move slowly and focus on form. On day one, count how many good squats you can do in 60 seconds.

Week 2 · Add a set

Do the full 3 sets. You should still have about two reps left at the end of each set.

Week 3 · Slow it down

Take 3 seconds to lower on every squat, lunge and push-up. Holds and planks: add 5 to 10 seconds.

Week 4 · Test yourself

Repeat week 3, then on your last day count your squats in 60 seconds again. Compare it with week 1.

Make it stick

Five habits for home training

The best home workout is the one you actually do. These habits make it easier to show up.

1. Same time, same place

Pick a time and a corner of the house. Put it in your calendar like an appointment.

2. Set up the night before

Leave your mat and shoes out. Fewer steps means fewer excuses.

3. Train with someone

A friend on a video call, or your kids joining in for the finisher.

4. Protein at every meal

Dal, paneer, curd, eggs, chicken, fish, sprouts or tofu. Aim for a palm-sized portion each meal.

5. Walk every day

7,000 to 8,000 steps. A 10-minute walk after meals is an easy start.

Busy day?

Do one round of each exercise in Workout C. Ten minutes still counts.
