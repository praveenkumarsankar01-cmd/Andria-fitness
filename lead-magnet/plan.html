Before you start

How to use this plan

Your week

Train three days a week with a rest day in between, for example Monday, Wednesday, Friday. Do Workout A, then B, then C.

On the other days, walk. Aim for 7,000 to 8,000 steps.

Pick the right weight

Choose a weight you can lift for every rep with good form, and that leaves two reps "in the tank". Most women start with 2 to 5 kg dumbbells.

No dumbbells? Use filled water bottles or a backpack with books, or do the bodyweight version.

Rest

Rest 60 to 90 seconds between sets. Breathe out as you push or pull.

Warm-up (5 minutes)

  • March on the spot, 1 minute
  • Arm circles, 30 seconds each way
  • Hip circles, 30 seconds each way
  • Bodyweight squats x 10
  • Glute bridges x 10
  • Cat-cow x 6

Cool-down (5 minutes)

  • Slow walk, 2 minutes
  • Hamstring stretch, 30 seconds each leg
  • Hip flexor stretch, 30 seconds each side
  • Chest stretch in a doorway, 30 seconds
  • Child's pose, 30 seconds

Safety first

Check with your doctor before you start, especially if you are pregnant or have a medical condition or injury. Stop if anything hurts (effort is fine, pain is not).

Week by week

Build a little each week

Week 1 · Learn the moves

Do 2 sets of each exercise instead of 3. Move slowly and focus on form. Write down the weights you use.

Week 2 · Add a set

Do the full 3 sets. Keep the same weights. You should still have about two reps left at the end of each set.

Week 3 · Add weight or reps

If the last set felt easy, go up one step in weight, or add 2 reps to each set. Planks and carries: add 5 to 10 seconds.

Week 4 · Test yourself

Repeat week 3, then on your last day do Workout A and count how many good goblet squats you can do in one set. Compare it with week 1.

Food habits

Five habits that matter most

No strict diet. Keep eating the food you like, and build these habits one at a time.

1. Protein at every meal

Dal, paneer, curd, eggs, chicken, fish, sprouts or tofu. Aim for a palm-sized portion each meal.

2. Half a plate of vegetables

At lunch and dinner. Any kind counts: poriyal, salad, sabzi, soup.

3. Water first

2 to 3 litres a day, more on training days and in the heat.

4. Sleep 7 hours

Poor sleep makes cravings and low energy worse. Keep a regular bedtime.

5. Walk every day

7,000 to 8,000 steps. A 10-minute walk after meals is an easy start.

Go easy on

Sugary drinks, fried snacks most days, and large late-night meals. Not banned, just less often.
