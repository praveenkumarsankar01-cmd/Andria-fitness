Before you start

How to use this plan

What toning really is

Toning is two things: a little more muscle and a little less fat. This plan builds the muscle; daily walks and the habits on page 5 help with the rest.

Your week

Train three days a week with a rest day in between, for example Monday, Wednesday, Friday. Do Workout A, then B, then C. On the other days, walk: 7,000 to 8,000 steps.

Pick the right weight

Choose a weight you can lift for every rep with good form, with two reps left "in the tank". Most women start with 2 to 5 kg. Rest 60 to 90 seconds between sets.

Warm-up (5 minutes)

  • March on the spot, 1 minute
  • Arm circles, 30 seconds each way
  • Hip circles, 30 seconds each way
  • Bodyweight squats x 10
  • Glute bridges x 10
  • Cat-cow x 6

Cool-down (5 minutes)

  • Slow walk, 2 minutes
  • Hamstring stretch, 30 seconds each leg
  • Hip flexor stretch, 30 seconds each side
  • Chest stretch in a doorway, 30 seconds
  • Child's pose, 30 seconds

Safety first

Check with your doctor before you start, especially if you are pregnant or have a medical condition or injury. Stop if anything hurts (effort is fine, pain is not).

Week by week

Build a little each week

Week 1 · Learn the moves

Do 2 sets of each exercise instead of 3. Move slowly and feel the muscle you are training. Take a photo and write down your weights.

Week 2 · Add a set

Do the full 3 sets with the same weights. You should still have about two reps left at the end of each set.

Week 3 · Slow the lowering

Take 3 seconds to lower on every rep. It is the quickest way to make the same weight work harder.

Week 4 · Add weight or reps

If the last set felt easy, go up one step in weight or add 2 reps. On your last day, take another photo in the same light and compare.

Food habits

Five habits that matter most

No strict diet. Keep eating the food you like, and build these habits one at a time.

1. Protein at every meal

Muscle is built from it. Dal, paneer, curd, eggs, chicken, fish, sprouts or tofu, a palm-sized portion each meal.

2. Half a plate of vegetables

At lunch and dinner. Any kind counts: poriyal, salad, sabzi, soup.

3. Water first

2 to 3 litres a day, more on training days and in the heat.

4. Sleep 7 hours

Muscle grows while you rest, and poor sleep makes cravings worse. Keep a regular bedtime.

5. Walk every day

7,000 to 8,000 steps. A 10-minute walk after meals is an easy start.

Go easy on

Sugary drinks, fried snacks most days, and large late-night meals. Not banned, just less often.
