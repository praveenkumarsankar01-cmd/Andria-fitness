FAQ

Good questions

Still unsure? Ask your coach on day one of your trial.

I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Yes. Most members start as beginners. Day one is a movement check, so every exercise is scaled to you.

Do I pay for the trial?

No. The 7 days are free and we don't ask for card details. If you want to continue, you pick a program after your review on day 7.

What's in the free plan?

12 workouts over 4 weeks, a warm-up and cool-down, a progress tracker and a one-page food habits guide. You need a pair of dumbbells or a gym.

Will I get a diet plan?

We coach simple food habits that fit everyday Indian meals, not strict diets. If you have a medical condition, we work alongside your doctor or dietitian.

I have an injury or a health condition.

Please check with your doctor first, and tell us in the trial form. Your coach will adapt or skip moves as needed.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Programs are monthly with no lock-in. Cancel before your next billing date and you won't be charged again.
